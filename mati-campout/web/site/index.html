MATI’s Campout

Keep the fire alive. Survive the night.

Chop wood, explore a beautiful pine forest, find the old lighthouse, tame a wolf, and keep your campfire burning when the dark comes creeping in. How many nights can you survive?

An island of light in an enormous forest

By day the forest is warm, golden and full of things to find. At night, your campfire is the only safe place for miles.

🔥

The campfire is everything

Warmth, light and safety. Feed it wood and coal. Watch the safe circle shrink as it burns low, and hurry when it goes out.

🌗

Day and night

Golden sunrises, long evening shadows and deep blue moonlit nights. Every night is a little harder than the last.

🪓

Gather and craft

Chop trees, collect stone and coal, craft better axes, and upgrade your tent through eight cozy levels.

🗺️

Explore

Trails, a misty lake, a rocky ridge, an abandoned camp, hidden treasure chests and a lighthouse on the far shore.

🐺

Tame a wolf

Offer a calm wolf a bone and gain a loyal friend who follows you, guards camp and helps you through the night.

👀

Things in the dark

Watchers stare from between the trees. Night Stalkers fear the light, and something much bigger is out there too.

How a day goes

Morning

Wake up, cook breakfast over the fire and plan your adventure.

Daytime

Explore, chop, gather, open chests and trade with friendly campers.

Sunset

The light turns gold. Head home before dark?

Night

Keep the fire fed, stay warm, and keep the creatures back.

Sunrise

You made it! One more night on your record.
